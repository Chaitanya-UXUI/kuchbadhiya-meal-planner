Design Principles
Always slightly rotated. Buttons, stickers & tags tilt ±1–2°. Nothing is perfectly straight — it's handmade.
3px bold borders. Cards, inputs and key containers have a thick Inkwell (#1a1a1a) border — the cutout collage feel.
Dashed = ghost / optional. Dashed borders signal actions that are optional or additive (ghost buttons, empty states).
Rainbow stripe as an accent. The 3-colour repeating stripe (Pink → Yellow → Blue) is used on top of cards, headers and dividers — never as full backgrounds.
Emoji as illustration. Food emojis replace custom icons on menu cards — fast, universal, and delightfully home-y.
Butter Cream for warmth. Use #FFFDE7 as an alternate card background to break monotony — like a sticky note on the fridge.
